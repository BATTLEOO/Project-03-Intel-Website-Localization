Sustainability Through the Ages

Explore Intel's journey through time — discovering how our commitment to innovation has shaped a more sustainable future for technology and our planet.

1968

Intel Founded

Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore rename NM Electronics to Intel Corporation, laying the foundation for decades of technological innovation.

1971

First Microprocessor

Intel debuts the 4004, the world's first commercial microprocessor, igniting the revolution and propelling the future of computing devices.

1978

8086 Processor

Launch of the 8086, establishing the x86 architecture that drives countless PCs and servers in the modern era.

1985

386 Processor

Intel introduces the 386 with 32-bit architecture, ushering in a new era of performance and multitasking for personal computers.

2006

Peak GHG Emissions

Intel's highest annual greenhouse gas emissions. The company then invests heavily in abatement, renewables, and efficient manufacturing to reverse this trend.

2020

RISE Strategy

Intel launches its RISE (Responsible, Inclusive, Sustainable, Enabling) strategy and 2030 goals, driving progress on climate action and waste reduction.

2022

Net-Zero By 2040

Intel commits to net-zero greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 1 and 2) across all global operations by 2040, building on years of environmental action.

2023

Renewable Electricity

Intel achieves 99% renewable electricity usage worldwide, drastically lowering carbon emissions and advancing its long-term sustainability goals.

2024

Sustainability Summit

Intel hosts its first Sustainability Summit, uniting suppliers, government officials, and industry leaders to collaborate on next-generation sustainable manufacturing.

Our Focus Areas

Pillars of Progress

Intel's RISE strategy is built on three pillars — each backed by measurable 2030 goals and transparent annual reporting.

🌍 Climate Action

Reduce absolute GHG emissions by 10% and achieve net-zero operational emissions by 2040 — targeting Scope 1, 2, and select Scope 3 sources.

Learn More →

💧 Water Stewardship

Restore 100% of global freshwater use and achieve net positive water impact by 2030, focusing on high water-stress regions where operations are concentrated.

Learn More →

♻️ Zero Waste

Send zero total waste to landfill from manufacturing and upcycle 90% of non-hazardous waste — extending product lifecycles and driving circular economy principles.
